# JNIT Product Catalogue Strategy

## Product principle

Do not sell a calculation that a free website performs in seconds. Sell a completed business outcome with saved state, validation, evidence, collaboration, exports, automation and accountable support.

## Applications worth validating

### Travellers Companion

Paid outcome: a verified trip workspace generated from fragmented travel documents.

Defensible value: extraction review, traveller collaboration, secure documents, calendar, family sharing and advisor workflows.

### Customs Workflow Platform

Paid outcome: auditable movement of customs packs from receipt through assignment, review and completion.

Defensible value: role-specific queues, evidence, turnaround reporting, controlled document exchange and customer visibility.

### IT Evidence Vault

Paid outcome: a small business can prove that backups, access reviews, recovery tests, incidents and supplier checks occurred.

Defensible value: recurring evidence requests, reminders, approvals, immutable history and audit-ready exports—not merely document storage.

### Client Handover Builder

Paid outcome: consultants and MSPs turn implementation records into a consistent operational handover pack.

Defensible value: guided collection, missing-evidence checks, architecture inventory, ownership matrix, runbooks and branded export.

### Recovery Readiness Workspace

Paid outcome: a company identifies critical services, recovery dependencies and untested assumptions, then records exercise evidence.

Defensible value: dependency mapping, RTO/RPO consistency checks, exercise scheduling, evidence and improvement tracking.

## Tools: free utility versus paid workflow

### Network Plan Builder

Keep CIDR arithmetic free. The paid workspace should add multiple environments, overlap and exhaustion detection, growth modelling, VLSM planning, saved plans, change history, exports and peer review.

### Authorised Network Evidence Scan

Do not sell another generic scanner. Build a controlled workflow only if it includes customer authorisation, private-network collection, inventory and change detection, ownership, risk explanations, remediation tasks and before/after evidence. Never offer anonymous internet-wide scanning.

### DNS and email security checker

Free checks can show SPF, DKIM, DMARC, DNSSEC and certificate status. Paid value should come from continuous monitoring, plain-language impact, guided remediation, history, reporting and regression alerts.

## Documents and store products

Generic templates are commodities. Sell guided, maintained implementation kits:

- Business continuity implementation kit
- Incident response and evidence kit
- Cloud migration decision pack
- Microsoft 365 governance starter pack
- IT service handover pack
- Backup and recovery test kit
- Vendor and access review kit

Each paid kit should include a guided questionnaire, editable working documents, fictional examples, decision guidance, registers, evidence sheets, version history and optional JNIT review.

The value is not making a document deliberately difficult. The value is helping the customer produce a defensible document that reflects their real business.

## Website structure

- **Services** — senior-led consulting and implementation
- **Applications** — hosted workflow products
- **Tools** — useful free diagnostics leading into paid workspaces
- **Store** — implementation kits, Terraform blueprints and document packs
- **Our work** — engineering evidence and portfolio projects

## Recommended build order

1. Publish the catalogue structure with honest `coming soon` and `private pilot` states.
2. Release two useful free tools: a CIDR calculator and DNS/email security check.
3. Validate Travellers Companion through paid concierge pilots.
4. Turn the strongest free tool into a saved workspace only after usage proves demand.
5. Launch one document kit with an optional paid review service.
