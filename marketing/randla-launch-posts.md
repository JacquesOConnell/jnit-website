## LinkedIn — personal share

I’m pleased to share that Randla, a personal budgeting and cash-flow app developed by JNIT Cloud Solutions, is now available on Google Play.

Randla was built around a simple idea: everyday money management should be clear, practical and respectful of your privacy. It supports budgets, bills, accounts, safe-to-spend guidance, reviewed statement imports, reports and encrypted backups, with the core experience working locally on the device.

This launch also represents the full product journey—from design and mobile development to testing, cloud-backed subscription verification and Google Play release management.

Android is live, and work on the iOS version is underway.

View Randla on Google Play:
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jnit.lifewallet
