Common questions

Straight answers before we begin.

How long does a website take?+

A focused one-page site may take 1–2 weeks. A professional multi-page website usually takes 3–5 weeks, depending on content, feedback and functionality.

Do I own the website?+

Yes. Once the agreed project balance is paid, the completed website belongs to your business. Third-party platforms and licences remain subject to their own terms.

Can you host and maintain it?+

Yes. Hosting, monitoring, updates and ongoing improvement can be included as a separate monthly care plan.

Can you build more than a normal website?+

Yes. JNIT also develops web applications, portals, dashboards, workflow tools and cloud integrations, quoted according to the required scope.
