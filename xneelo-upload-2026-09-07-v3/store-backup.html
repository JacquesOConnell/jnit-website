Simple ordering

Stock confirmed before you pay.

Every order is handled personally so that the product, upgrades, price and delivery method are agreed before payment.

01

Choose

Select a product and note the reference shown on its listing.

02

Send a request

Email store@jnit.co.za or use WhatsApp. Include the product reference and any RAM or SSD upgrade you would like.

03

Confirm stock

JNIT checks current availability with the supplier and gets back to you with the grade, final configuration and total price.

04

Accept and pay

If you accept, JNIT sends the invoice and banking details. Your order proceeds once cleared payment reflects in JNIT's bank account.

05

Prepare and deliver

The supplier checks and prepares the computer, installs the licensed operating system and relevant drivers, and completes the agreed configuration. JNIT then arranges collection, courier, or local delivery within the agreed 20 km service radius.

Payment verification

A proof of payment or payment notification is not sufficient on its own. Goods will not be released and no courier or delivery will be booked until cleared payment reflects in JNIT's bank account.

Courier estimate: Delivery outside Gauteng usually takes 3–5 working days after dispatch. Courier timeframes are estimates and may be affected by destination or carrier delays.

Important refurbished-device information

Availability: Supplier stock can change without notice. Availability is confirmed before payment.

Grades: Grades indicate cosmetic condition. Individual devices may have visible signs of previous use.

Images: Catalogue visuals are representative. The appearance of the specific unit may vary.

Battery: Battery performance varies on refurbished devices. Its disclosed refurbished condition does not remove rights that apply under consumer law.

Warranty: Applicable statutory consumer rights are not limited by the supplier's warranty. Written assessment, remedy and return terms are provided with the quotation.
